AI Creation

See how AI systems are built, step by step.

This track turns the hidden mechanics of AI into learning labs: data is broken into pieces, information moves through a network, and a model adjusts itself based on feedback.

1. Tokenization

Break text into pieces the model can understand.

A language model does not read a whole sentence as one blob. It splits text into tokens, sometimes by word and sometimes by smaller subword chunks.

2. Forward pass

Watch the signal move through a simple network.

The forward pass takes input values, multiplies them by weights, adds bias, and passes the result through an activation function.

3. Backpropagation

Adjust the model when it makes a mistake.

Backpropagation compares the prediction to the target and updates the weights to reduce the error. This is how a model learns over time.
