AI Usage

Learn how people use AI well, not just how it works.

The strongest AI workflows combine model capability with judgment, context, and ethical care. This track explores how to ask better questions and use AI responsibly.

1. Prompt engineering

Turn a vague question into a useful AI instruction.

A strong prompt gives the model a role, clear task, constraints, and a desired output style. That helps the answer become more reliable and easier to use.

2. AI ethics

Use AI with awareness of fairness, privacy, and impact.

Good AI use is not only about getting a better answer. It is also about asking whether the answer is fair, accurate, safe, and appropriate for the real-world situation.

3. Research ideas

Turn curiosity into a project question.

Research-like thinking often starts with a discussion question: what is the trade-off, what is the cause, and how could we test it?

Research question: How does prompt structure affect the quality of AI-generated explanations?
