How we work

Explore AI, build projects, and grow with confidence.

Tech League is built around helping students explore AI in a real, hands-on way. We mix teaching, collaboration, and creativity so members can learn quickly and pursue projects that feel meaningful to them.

  • Intro workshops on AI, ethics, and real-world use cases
  • Project tracks like Medium articles, mini tools, and research
  • Mentorship, feedback, and team-based experimentation
  • Hackathon prep, showcases, and student-led events
