About us

We help students explore AI in a way that feels practical and empowering.

Tech League is a student-led club focused on making AI approachable. We support members as they learn the basics, build projects, and participate in events like hackathons, research showcases, and writing opportunities.

Our mission

We want students to feel confident learning AI, exploring how it works, and finding a way to contribute through the kind of work they enjoy most—writing, building, coding, or presenting.

What we do

We run workshops, project-based learning sessions, and student opportunities that connect AI concepts to real work such as Medium articles, mini tools, and hackathon prep.

Our values

Built around curiosity, teamwork, and execution.
